## 🎨 Visual Identity & Brand Archetype

- **Brand Name:** SnapEdge AI
- **Tagline:** Autonomous On-Device Intelligence Workstation
- **Archetype:** High-Tech Neural Hardware Accelerator & Cybersecurity Guardian
- **Core Visual Metaphors:**
  1. **Hexagonal NPU Core**: Represents the **45 TOPS Qualcomm Hexagon™ NPU** and dedicated on-device neural hardware acceleration.
  2. **Edge Lightning Nexus**: Depicts lightning-fast zero-latency inference and real-time power governance (11.2x watts/token efficiency).
  3. **Neural Circuit Node Traces**: Symbolizes multi-agent concurrency (Workspace, Voice Scribe, Neural RAG, Vision Shield) operating air-gapped on NPU SRAM.
